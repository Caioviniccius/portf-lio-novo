FAQ

Questions every client asks

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your business needs: number of pages, menu, booking, store or dashboard. After a quick chat, you get a proposal with a fixed price, no surprises.

How long does it take?

The timeline also depends on the size of the project and is stated in the proposal before we start.

Does the website work well on mobile?

Yes. Every project is designed mobile-first, since that is how most of your customers will visit.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

If you need it, the website can come with a simple dashboard to change products, prices, photos and promotions without coding.

Do I need to understand domains and hosting?

No. I'll guide you through the technical side and leave your website published and running.
