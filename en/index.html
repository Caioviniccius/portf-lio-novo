How it works

From first message to launch

You'll know what you're getting, what it costs and when it will be ready before any work begins.

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We talk about your business, your customers and what the website needs to do. This can all happen by email or chat.

  2. 2

    Proposal

  3. 3

    Development

  4. 4

    Delivery

FAQ

Common questions

What types of websites do you build?

Business websites, landing pages, online menus with ordering, booking systems, online stores and simple admin dashboards so you can update content yourself. You can see examples of each in my work.

How does the project process work?

There are four steps: discovery, proposal, development and delivery. You approve the scope and price before any work begins, and you review the website before it goes live.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope: the number of pages, features such as booking or an online store, and whether you need a dashboard. Once I understand what you need, I send a proposal with a fixed price for that scope.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. The estimated timeline is included in the proposal, before we start.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. I can rebuild an existing website with a modern design that works well on phones. Send me the link and tell me what you would like to improve.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every project is designed mobile-first, since that is how most of your customers will visit.

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?

I publish the website and guide you through registering and connecting your domain, which stays in your name. Ongoing maintenance and updates after delivery can be arranged separately.

How do payments work?

The payment method and schedule are defined in each proposal, before the project starts.

Can we communicate by email or chat?

Yes. We can talk by email or WhatsApp, whichever you prefer. Written communication works well because every decision stays documented. I'm based in Brazil (UTC−3), which overlaps with business hours across the US.
