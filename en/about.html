About

Websites that help small businesses grow

I'm Caio Vinícius, a web developer from Brazil. I build websites for small and medium-sized businesses such as restaurants, barbershops, clinics, gyms and retail stores, focused on making things easy for their customers: browsing a menu, placing an order, booking an appointment or finding a product in a few taps, right from their phone.

Every project is custom-built around your brand and designed to load fast. Depending on what you need, your website can include online ordering, booking, an online store, WhatsApp integration and a simple dashboard to update products, prices and promotions on your own.

I work remotely and communicate in writing by email or WhatsApp, so I can work with clients anywhere.

How I work

  1. 1

    Discovery

    We talk about your business, your customers and what the website needs to do. This can all happen by email or chat.

  2. 2

    Proposal

    You receive a written proposal with the scope, timeline and price. Work only starts once you approve it.

  3. 3

    Development

    I build the website and share progress along the way so you can review it and request changes.

  4. 4

    Delivery

    After your final approval, I publish the website and help you set up your domain, as agreed in the proposal.
